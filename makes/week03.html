A Funny Comic?

An experiment with AI-generated humor, comic structure, and human editing.

The Comic

Reflection

With this comic, I wanted to explore AI's ability to tell jokes in a narrative format. Humor-based comics often involve closely relating visuals with witty and balanced dialogue, which I doubted the AI would be able to fully accomplish. The outcome was as I expected. While the joke of having a bad superpower made some sense, there wasn't any grounded humor in the comic. The short visuals the AI suggested, such as "Panel 5 Money flying everywhere. Chaos.", felt that they were made around the story, instead of actually adding onto the story. They didn't expand on the joke, and only helped the reader visualize it.

In addition, some of the visuals did not make sense for a comic format. For example, the AI suggested that panel 3 had the toast falling in slow motion, and then a splat sound effect as it hit the ground, but that kind of movement cannot happen in one panel. This helped me realize that AI can see patterns and comedy formats, but has a hard time making a real, grounded joke that uses multiple layers of context to connect with the reader. This left the comic feeling uninteresting, and the joke seeming weak and lacking wit. I felt that the comic needed more human control in the making of it in order to better connect with an audience.

Attribution

  • AI Tool: ChatGPT (dialogue, plot)
  • Human Contribution: Panel structure, drawings, editing

One of Many GIF

A looping GIF about individuality, repetition, and AI-generated crowds.

Project Details

  • Title: One of Many
  • Tools Used: ChatGPT (image generation), Photopea (editing, GIF creation)

The Artifact

Reflection

Allowing this image to repeat gave the emotion it conveyed more impact and meaning. With its loop, it identified where in the crowd the nondescript person was, and then showed how irrelevant they were in the bigger picture. This repetition placed emphasis on the insignificance of individuality, and took the final product into something that went beyond its initial image. The human choice that went into how this image looped is what gave the GIF meaning. Without the animation or text, the image is empty--it's only an AI-generated photo of a crowd. The human decision made it into something that could be used as a relatable reaction. By identifying one person in the crowd and then zooming out, it shows a feeling of believing you are unique, while actually being the same as everyone else. This feeling cannot be conveyed with the image alone.

Even though the GIF is given meaning by human creativity and decisions, there are still examples of automation shown in it. I prompted ChatGPT to give me a large crowd, and it defaulted to producing a sea of white men. After some refining, it generated an image with a greater variance in people, while still keeping them nondescript. Still, the choice to make most members of the crowd white and male, even in the final result, highlights AI's pattern-based choices. This AI usage complicates the originality of the work because the base of the GIF is entirely AI generated, limiting my control over the kinds of people displayed. Although the GIF's meaning came from my choices, I believe that the GIF is not entirely mine because part of the authorship and art came from ChatGPT. However, the most important part of the GIF is the meaning that is portrayed, which came from my decisions; AI added a layer of coauthorship, but the most significant pieces of the work come from my ideas.

Attribution & AI Use

  • AI Tool Used: ChatGPT
  • Prompt (summary): "Create an image showing someone in a crowd, who blends in with everyone else there."
  • What the AI Generated: A photo-realistic image of a small, all white, mostly male crowd.
  • What I Changed or Remixed: I further prompted the AI to "make it a graphic with less specific features" and "make the crowd look a little different, with a mix of people but no one really stands out, and bigger." I then took that image and turned it into an animation, adding text and deciding on each frame.
  • Additional Tools: Photopea for layout and GIF creation.

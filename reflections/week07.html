AI, Power, Labor, and Geography

Prompt

How does AI reshape global geographies of power, labor, and environment?

Reflection

AI reshapes global geographies by centralizing the choice of who is impacted by AI onto certain companies. Those who make the decisions about AI are the ones who hold power over how labor is distributed and how the environment is changed. AI data centers can introduce jobs to an area, but also impact its water quality and strain its power grid. Companies can offer AI services to certain regions, like the US, while outsourcing mining and mineral extraction to countries like Peru and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Those choices affect global supply chains and the economies of entire regions, even those far from where the companies are located. These centralized tech companies decide who is benefited and who is harmed by AI, where jobs open and where communities suffer from resource demand.

Imagining AI Futures

AI Futures

Prompt

Do you imagine AI futures as utopian, dystopian, or in-between? What do your visions reveal about us today?

Reflection

I imagine AI futures as something in-between utopian and dystopian, because the growth and use of AI is something that we have control over. Because I have faith that people will continue to care, faith in activists, and (some) faith in policymakers, I think that we will not allow AI to create a dystopian future. There may be many aspects of the future that I don't like or hope to not come true, but I think a true dystopia can only happen if the power of the people and the way our world works now goes completely out the window. This prediction reveals that today people have a lot of choice in how governments make decisions, and can call for change and spread awareness of AI's harms before anything becomes truly terrible.

I also think that a utopia is impossible. AI will always raise privacy and ethical concerns, from the exploitative nature of how it is created to the way it uses and collects data. Exploitation has existed for centuries, and will continue with the use of AI, so a perfect utopia is unachievable. This reveals that I don't see AI as going away, especially because of how integrated it is into our lives now.

As a result, AI future will be something in-between--always there, but something that we have adapted to and simply creates a different way of life. It might be involved in our medical decisions, in surveillance, and our jobs, but we have enough control and enough people who understand the harms that it likely won't ruin our world.

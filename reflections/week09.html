Creativity Within Constraint

Bot Creativity

Prompt

What is creativity when AI can generate endlessly? Where is the human in generative work? What was your experience building the bot? Were you in control of the output?

Reflection

When AI can generate endlessly, creativity only comes from the kinds of constraints and challenges we give it. When I made my bot, I found that the only outputs that were interesting or engaging were ones that resulted from creative inputs that pushed my bot to break its rules. When I refused to engage with the bot, there was little creativity in its outputs--without the human push or editing, it just generated the same loop of content. Therefore, the human part of generative work is the guidelines and rules that we provide to either shape the bot or push it farther.

My control of the output of my Character.ai bot varied. When chatting, many of my edits only redirected the bot--I wasn't always able to fully control it. It would listen to some suggestions, such as following my choice of conversation or preferred speaking tone. However, other times it would make its own decisions and be harder to control. For example, it would answer me with very flirty dialogue even after I told it to stop. This may be able to be controlled in the original prompt defining the bot's personality, but it limited my curation while chatting.

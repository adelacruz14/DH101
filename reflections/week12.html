AI Sustainability and Ecological Trade-Offs

Sustainability

Prompt

Is AI sustainable? What ecological trade-offs are we willing to accept?

Reflection

AI is not sustainable because of the resource demands that its training and maintenance put on the environment. AI companies require consistently increasing amounts of water, land, power usage, and CO2 emissions. Within the current system, this is not sustainable, and cannot be until we are able to environmentally offset these costs with sustainable green energy choices.

We're willing to accept a number of ecological trade-offs in all of the choices that we make. By deciding to drive to work, fly on planes, support unethical farming practices, and more, we are deciding to hurt the environment for our own gain. As long as we feel disconnected from those impacts, we can make those decisions easily. So as long as AI companies continue abstracting the impacts they have on the environment by disconnecting the user from the hardware and resource demand, we are willing to accept any ecological trade-off. This is demonstrated by the widespread use of AI despite its known impacts, like when people ask AI for things that they could Google (which would take a little more effort but a fraction of the environmental impact).
